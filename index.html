Questions

Good to know

How do I book a session?

Tap any WhatsApp button on this page, or use the form below. Share your topic and you will get a reply to arrange a time.

What do I need for a horoscope or birth star reading?

Your date, exact time and place of birth. If you are unsure of the time, mention it and discuss it in the chat.

What is shared for a Vastu consultation?

Start by describing your home or workplace on WhatsApp. Details such as the layout and direction of the property will be discussed there.

Is this a substitute for professional advice?

No. Guidance is offered for inspiration and self-reflection, and does not replace medical, legal or financial advice.
